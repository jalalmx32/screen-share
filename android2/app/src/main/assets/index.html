📖 Tutorial

Follow these steps to set up screen sharing:

1
📶 Enable Hotspot
Turn on WiFi hotspot on your Android device. Make sure the hotspot is active and broadcasting.
2
📶 Connect PC
Connect your PC to the Android hotspot. Go to WiFi settings on your PC and select the hotspot network.
3
💻 Start Server
Run the ScreenShare server application on your Windows PC. It will start listening for connections.
4
🔍 Auto Detect
The app will automatically scan for available servers on the network. You can also manually enter the IP address.
5
🖐 Touch & Pinch
Tap to click, long press for right-click, pinch to zoom in/out, and drag to pan around the screen.
6
⌨ Keyboard
Full QWERTY keyboard with special keys (Win, Alt, Ctrl, Shift, Tab, etc.) for complete PC control.
7
📋 Clipboard Sync
Use the Clipboard feature to send and receive text between your Android device and PC seamlessly.

⚠ Troubleshooting

⚠
Connection Issues?
- Make sure both devices are on the same network
- Check that the PC firewall allows connections
- Try restarting both the server and this app
- Verify the IP address is correct
